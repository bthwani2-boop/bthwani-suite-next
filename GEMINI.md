# Gemini CLI Adapter

Use `governance/GOVERNANCE.md` as the unified repository governance entry point. `AGENTS.md` is the thin coding-agent adapter; resolve execution and authority conflicts through the canonical orchestrator and the applicable Product/System Truth.

Pin the exact user-named branch and commit. Read `governance/product/PRD.md` and only the applicable general policy/Product Truth. Use `.agents/INDEX.md` only when routing is not obvious, and load a policy under `.agents/tools/` only after selecting that conditional tool.

Planning artifacts are not policy or evidence and live outside the repository. Graphify, LeanCTX, OpenCodeReview, Nx, runtime environments, and full verification suites are optional and evidence-driven. This adapter creates no policy, product truth, or approval.
