Status: ACTIVE_CANONICAL_ORCHESTRATOR_OWNER
Owner: exact-h evidence, falsification, deletion proof, admission proof, fresh full recensus and Level-4 fixed-point qualification.

## 1. Evidence does not define architecture

CI, tests, scanners, linters, builds, runtime probes and reviews are evidence producers only.

```text
GREEN != CANONICAL
GREEN != CLOSED
ZERO_EXIT != FINDINGS_DISPOSITIONED
UPLOADED_ARTIFACT != EVIDENCE_CONSUMED
```

Never turn a failed check directly into the next patch without causal classification.

## 7. Admission-hole proof

For every material defect prove both:

```text
DEFECT REMOVED
+
SYSTEM THAT ADMITTED/ACCEPTED/FAILED TO DETECT IT REFOUNDED OR PROVEN CORRECT
```

Use the simplest durable prevention capable of falsifying recurrence: schema constraint, type system, contract generation, compiler, test, runtime assertion, static/security check, CI gate, or canonical ownership structure.

Do not create governance machinery merely to validate governance prose.
