# U003 — cart, serviceability, checkout and exactly-once order creation

This is the highest-risk client command boundary because several authoritative decisions meet in one user action. Cart state and commercial snapshots are DSH truth; address and active geofence come from DSH; zone/SLA/capacity/delivery-mode policy remains a DSH capability whose Product Truth is still in `DISCOVERY`; payment allocation and money movement remain WLT truth. None of these facts may be replaced by a frontend status, cached eligibility result or optimistic checkout state.

The correction order is deliberately transactional. First prove the owned cart and its version/idempotency semantics. Then prove one current serviceability/fulfillment decision is evaluated from canonical policy and revalidated at the command boundary. Finally prove a valid checkout intent creates at most one order under repeated or concurrent submit, captures immutable item/price/currency/address/fulfillment snapshots, and emits any required operational/outbox effect with the same correlation discipline. A stale or denied policy result must not be converted into success because the client previously displayed an enabled button.

WLT enters only for server-owned payment allocation, eligibility/reference and financial effects. DSH may orchestrate or persist bounded references but cannot become a wallet, ledger, payment or refund owner. Ambiguous external/financial outcomes remain unknown/reconcilable rather than retried through another route or shown as paid.

The unit includes the control-panel platform surface only where an operator policy write changes the exact serviceability decision consumed by cart/checkout. Independent platform administration is outside scope. Closure requires frontend contracts, targeted Go tests including race-sensitive owner packages, PostgreSQL invariants, API/financial boundary guards and exact-candidate runtime evidence.
