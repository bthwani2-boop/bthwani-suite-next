# U012 — cross-surface-cleanup-red-team-e2e-closure

This unit is deliberately last and contains no new product authority. Its job is to prove that the preceding units converge as one Captain product rather than as isolated green components. Cross-surface scope is narrow: partner only where it writes/reads Captain fleet or custody/proof, client only where it reads Captain-caused delivery/tracking/proof state, and control-panel only for Captain lifecycle/dispatch/tracking/support/finance operations. Independent journeys on those surfaces remain outside even if a global CI job contains them.

Cleanup is part of closure. The final candidate must have no proven dead Captain routes, stale aliases, fabricated demonstration data, contradictory copy, unused imports/exports/config/dependencies, duplicate truth paths, broad any-casts that conceal Captain contract defects, or native permission residue without a product capability. Search/caller evidence must accompany deletion so a valid integration is not removed merely because it looks old.

The red-team pass assumes the system is wrong and attacks actor isolation, wrong role/surface, stale session, cancellation/reassignment, concurrent accept/handoff/capacity, replay/idempotency, weak network, restart, permission revocation, stale location/media, support deep links, financial duplicates/provider uncertainty and operator readback. Required checks are rerun after the final code write. Package bookkeeping is written only after product evidence, then the candidate is repinned and strict closure validation is run. Any movement or failure reopens the affected unit.

## Closure boundary

Final closure is allowed only when all Captain-bounded owners and consumers agree on the exact final candidate and no known fixable Captain defect or cleanup residue remains. The unit remains open until all required checks are executed on the exact candidate, all known fixable Captain defects in this concern are removed, cleanup is complete, and later evidence has not invalidated the candidate.
