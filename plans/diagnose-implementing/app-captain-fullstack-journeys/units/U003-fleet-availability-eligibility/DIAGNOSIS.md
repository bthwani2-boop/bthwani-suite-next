# U003 — fleet, availability and dispatch eligibility

Partner-fleet membership remains a DSH-owned versioned lifecycle. The Captain may redeem a valid single-use connection code, list only their own memberships across stores, and disconnect only their own membership. Partner and operator surfaces read the same persisted lifecycle with role-appropriate redaction. Digest-only secret storage, expiry/revocation, optimistic versions, audit and notifications are not optional supporting details; a failure in any of them leaves fleet truth open.

The major rebaseline change is dispatch eligibility. Current `assignment_governance.go` does not select candidates from availability and capacity alone. A Captain candidate must be accredited/approved, marked available, within remaining active-assignment capacity, have a current unexpired WLT financial-eligibility projection, have a sufficiently fresh dispatch profile, have no active provider absence, and be evaluated for the required service-area context. During assignment validation DSH locks the Captain profile row and recomputes active capacity inside the transaction so concurrent offers cannot consume the same capacity slot.

This creates a cross-owner boundary that must remain clean: WLT decides financial eligibility and DSH consumes that projection as an authorization input; DSH must not recreate financial arithmetic. Workforce/provider absence truth may affect dispatch eligibility but the frontend must not synthesize a final decision. UI availability/service-mode controls are intents or readbacks only.

The root-cause risks are stale or partial eligibility models, race-prone capacity, and separate fleet/readiness views that drift across Captain, partner and operator. U003 therefore requires database and backend evidence, not a string search alone. Any changed eligibility rule must update contract/readback/reason-code behavior for every affected Captain/operator consumer and remove obsolete client-side final eligibility logic. The unit remains open while any known mismatch, untested concurrency branch, stale projection handling defect or secret/isolation issue is known within this scope.
