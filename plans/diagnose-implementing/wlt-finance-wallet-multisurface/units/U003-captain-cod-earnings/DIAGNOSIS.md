# U003 Diagnosis — Captain COD and Earnings

Captain COD is the most important incompatible legacy path. Current WLT COD records are modeled as custody collection and later remittance, including captain collectors. The canonical Product Truth now requires a funded captain wallet path: financial authorization is order-specific and atomic, concurrent assignments cannot consume the same available capacity, cancellation releases exactly once, and finalization debits exactly once. It additionally forbids the same order value from also creating a second remittance liability. Therefore this is not a cosmetic status rename; active captain COD writer/reader semantics must migrate.

The unit must preserve historical auditability. Existing `pending_collection/collected/remitted` rows cannot be rewritten into a fictional funded-wallet history. They should remain historical/read-only or be mapped through explicit compatibility projections while new captain orders use the canonical reserve/finalize model. Any alternate custody model for store courier or partner store must not be silently deleted or kept: its active future requires an explicit Product Truth proving that it is a distinct economic effect. Until then, prevent it from contaminating captain-funded accounting.

Captain delivery earnings and field/other commissions also intersect COD because the delivery fee must never be counted twice and future unearned amounts cannot increase COD capacity. Earnings should post only at their authoritative earning event through the ledger, with source/order idempotency. The app may display one wallet balance while WLT internally tracks held/pending/earned/withdrawal eligibility as projections.
