# U007 Diagnosis — Commercial, Promotion Funding and Pricing Controls

Financially material WLT modules were absent from the old package. Store onboarding fee policy uses `float64`, singleton `id=1` and lacks durable audit in the current implementation. Subscription purchase/renewal/compensation must prove exact payment/refund/ledger lineage. Promotion funding accepts operatorContextId from payload/header and records funded amounts/commit/reverse without proving trusted scope plus one canonical accounting effect. Pricing quote accepts unit price and fee inputs; each money input must have a governed authoritative source, integer bounds and exact PaymentAllocation binding. The current DSH checkout now consumes canonical minor-unit snapshots; preserve that change and prove its lineage rather than reverting to legacy monetary fields. This unit covers only these finance concerns, not general catalog/marketing work.
