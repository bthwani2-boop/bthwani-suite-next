# Diagnosis — Cross-surface Journey Closure

This unit is the final proof layer for the entire Control Panel. The derived section registry and journey registry show that operator actions participate in multi-surface journeys; they are used for discovery completeness but cannot replace canonical code/contracts. Completion therefore requires candidate-bound scenarios that begin with an authorized operator read or mutation and follow the committed consequence into every directly affected surface and owner. A page rendering successfully is not closure.

Verification must cover forbidden access, object/scope isolation, search and bulk safety, conflict/concurrency, duplicate submission, response loss, session expiry/actor change, dependency degradation and recovery. Each state-changing scenario must prove the canonical owner and durable readback; financial scenarios must prove WLT ownership and operational scenarios DSH ownership. Client/Partner/Captain/Field compatibility must be checked only on linked journeys so execution does not expand into unrelated mobile features. Loading, success, empty, error, no-results and forbidden states plus RTL/accessibility must be exercised where applicable. Final closure is allowed only when all unit checks pass on one exact candidate SHA with no unresolved blockers or deviations.
