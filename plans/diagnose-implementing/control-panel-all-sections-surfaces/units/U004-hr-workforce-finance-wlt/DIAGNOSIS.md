# Diagnosis — HR/Workforce and WLT Finance

This unit joins two high-risk operator domains only at the Control Panel boundary while preserving separate canonical owners. HR/workforce controls affect Field and Captain eligibility, profile/status and operational access; finance controls affect wallets, settlement, commissions, payout readiness and COD. The WLT route exists under the Control Panel runtime, but WLT frontend composition is shared rather than a separate `frontend/control-panel` source. That is a deliberate boundary: monetary truth must stay WLT-owned and must not be reimplemented in DSH or in the browser.

Implementation must verify identity/workforce authorization and state gates before downstream operational use. Financial mutations require server-side actor/object authorization, trusted scope, idempotency, immutable/append-safe ledger semantics, reconciliation/readback and audit. Payout destination or financial verification changes must handle sensitive data without leaking raw values into inappropriate DSH surfaces. Captain, Field, Partner and Client are included only for directly affected financial/workforce readbacks. Any DSH finance module is treated as a governed bridge/consumer; it must not become a competing ledger or settlement source.
