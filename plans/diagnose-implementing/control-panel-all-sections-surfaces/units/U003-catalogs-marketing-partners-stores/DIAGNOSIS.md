# Diagnosis — Catalogs, Marketing, Partners and Stores

This unit covers the commercial/publication path controlled from Catalogs, Marketing and Partners, including nested Partner/Store detail flows that a top-level navigation inventory can miss. Partner onboarding/approval, Store readiness/publication, catalog/category/product governance, availability and marketing visibility are coupled because a committed operator decision can immediately change what the Client sees and what the Partner can manage. Field is included where onboarding evidence or partner setup feeds an operator approval/readback.

The implementation must identify canonical DSH owners and remove any parallel UI/BFF truth rather than copying state between panels. Approval and publication actions need object authorization, trusted Store/Partner scope, idempotent or conflict-safe semantics, audit evidence and canonical readback. Search and bulk catalog/partner actions require per-object scope enforcement. Client-visible publication must not be inferred optimistically from an operator response; the downstream Client/Partner state must be re-read from committed DSH truth. Marketing gates may consume catalog/store state but must not silently become the source of Store or catalog truth. Every nested/detail route must inherit the same authorization and failure semantics as its list page.
