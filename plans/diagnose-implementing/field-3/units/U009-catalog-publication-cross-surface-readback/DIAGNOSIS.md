# U009 — Central Catalog, Marketing publication and cross-surface readback

Field is not a catalog authority. It may choose approved Master Products, set store-local price/availability/description and submit product/correction/image proposals; it never edits Master truth directly. The existing central-catalog direction is therefore preserved. What changes is publication ownership: the approved business decision assigns store publication to Marketing in Control Panel. Generic Marketing functionality remains outside scope, but the publication command, gate and override are causally in scope.

The default path is an automatic server-evaluated Composite Publication Gate. Marketing may perform a documented audited override only where policy permits; no mobile or Partner UI can publish by itself. Product visibility follows master approval plus store assortment publication conditions. A critical expired document may block/hide only according to policy, and suspension of the Field provider after successful onboarding does not automatically hide approved stores. Closure requires the same `storeId` and `productId` through Field creation, Control Panel review, Partner readback and Client positive/negative visibility.
